This matrix binds language to evidence. “Allowed after gate” is not a claim
that the gate has already passed.

## Primary claim ceiling

Only after the corresponding gates pass:

> We introduce a source-conditioned, region-aware and grammar-constrained
> continuous-time Edit Flow that generates diverse and contractually legal
> minimal edits for 5′ and 3′ UTRs, and evaluate its controllability and
> transfer under matched-budget generative and search baselines.

No C0 or D0 artifact establishes this claim. These phases only freeze the
question, evidence language, execution contract, and data qualification paths.
